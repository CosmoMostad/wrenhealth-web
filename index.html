Features

Everything your family needs,
in one secure place.

From everyday medication tracking to emergency situations — Wren Health gives you the information you need, exactly when you need it.

Complete Health Profiles

Store blood type, allergies, medications, medical history, insurance details, and emergency contacts for every family member.

The Whole Family

Add family members of any age — children, parents, grandparents. One account covers everyone, with distinct profiles for each person.

Controlled Sharing

Share specific sections of a health profile with doctors, caregivers, or family members. You define exactly what they can access and for how long.

Family Health Calendar

Coordinate appointments and checkups for every family member in a shared calendar. Receive reminders before every visit — for yourself and those you care for.

Quick-Access Contacts

Save your physicians' office numbers and pharmacy details directly on each profile. One tap to call — no searching required during a stressful moment.

Document Storage

Attach medical records, lab results, prescription labels, and insurance cards directly to each profile. Everything in one place, always with you.

Built for caregivers.

Managing a parent's medications, specialists, and appointments is hard. Wren Health makes it organized, shareable, and accessible — for the whole care team.

Ready in an emergency.

When a family member can't speak for themselves, every second counts. Pull up a complete health profile in seconds — allergies, medications, blood type, emergency contacts.

Privacy & Security

Your health data stays yours.

We built Wren Health with privacy at the foundation — not as a feature. Your family's most sensitive information deserves the highest level of protection, and you remain in control of every piece of it.

Encrypted at Rest and in Transit

Your data is encrypted end-to-end. No one — including us — can read it without your credentials.

Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

Built on infrastructure designed for healthcare compliance and reliability.

No Data Selling. Ever.

We will never sell, license, or share your health data with insurers, advertisers, or third parties.

You Control Every Share

Choose what to share, with whom, and revoke access at any time. No exceptions.
